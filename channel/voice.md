# voice.md

Voice profile for the channel. The yt-* skills read this file (in this repo it replaces `~/.claude/youtube/voice.md`, see CLAUDE.md). Lines marked DEFAULT are proposals by Claude that the owner has not confirmed yet. Lines marked OWNER TO FILL need real input; nothing there is guessed.

## Who I am talking to

From the channel decisions: complete beginners who are curious about AI, plus anyone who wants to think and learn better. They are watching a vertical Short, often on mute, and decide in about 1.5 seconds whether to stay. One person at a time: "you", never "guys" or "everyone".

OWNER TO FILL: a sharper picture of one real viewer (age, situation, what they already tried).

## How I actually talk

The channel is faceless: the voice is the narration, not a person on camera.

DEFAULT narration style: calm, plain and friendly. Short sentences, one idea each, about 150 words per minute, second person. Say the thing, then show it. One clear example instead of a list of three.

OWNER TO FILL: if you have a narrator voice, a language level (simple English for learners, or everyday native English) or a video you like the sound of, put it here so scripts match it.

## Words I never use

DEFAULT list, change freely: "unlock", "game-changer", "dive in", "let's get into it", "revolutionary", "mind-blowing", "secret" (unless it is literally a secret), "hack" in medical or money contexts, "guys".

## What I will not claim

Taken from the channel rules:
- No numbers, studies, results or sources I cannot show. Every claim in Mind Hacks and Learn Faster is checked against its source first.
- No medical, legal or financial advice, no diagnosing the viewer.
- No promises like "this always works" or "everyone does this". Results vary.
- No photoreal AI footage presented as real. Animations and screen demos only.
- No tool demo that I did not actually run: on-screen AI answers are real outputs, shortened, never written by hand.

## My format

- Faceless Shorts in English, 9:16, 45 to 60 seconds, posted Monday to Friday, one video per day.
- No intro, no channel greeting, no logo sting. The hook is the first words and the first frame.
- Burned-in captions, large, inside the platform safe zone.
- One ask per video, normally "save this". No subscribe or follow pitch.
- Series: AI in 60, Mind Hacks, Learn Faster. Same palette and type across all videos, a new visual metaphor in each.
