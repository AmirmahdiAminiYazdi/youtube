# 01 - 3 Lines That Make AI Useful

Series: AI in 60. Target post date: Tue 2026-11-03. Format: 9:16 Short, about 50 seconds. Status: script drafted, waiting for owner approval (no voiceover generated yet).

## The one sentence it promises

A beginner can get a useful answer from any AI tool by adding three short lines to a vague question.

- Captions burned in, large, one line at a time. Keep all text inside the safe zone (about 12 percent margin top and bottom for platform UI).
