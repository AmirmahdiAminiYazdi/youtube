# 09 - Stop Rereading

Series: Learn Faster. Target post date: Thu 2026-11-05 (calendar slot for #9). Format: 9:16 Short, about 50 seconds. Status: script drafted, waiting for owner approval of hook and script (no voiceover generated yet).

## The one sentence it promises

Rereading your notes is one of the weakest ways to study, and closing the notes and recalling from memory is one of the strongest.

## The original element (required, do not skip)

A worked recall example on screen, labelled "Example". Topic: the four stages of the water cycle. The recall box shows what a student might write: evaporation, condensation, precipitation. After "opening the notes" the fourth stage, collection, is the one marked as missed. This is an illustration of the method, not a result: do not say or show that it improved anyone's score, and do not present it as a real student. The facts used are plain textbook facts. Use any other simple topic if you prefer, but check the facts.
