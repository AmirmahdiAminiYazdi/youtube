## Simplifications (named, not hidden)

- "Rain seeps down" stands for surface water and runoff in general.
- Screens are used in loose, sandy ground. Many bedrock wells stay open hole, with no screen. The narration says "in sandy ground", which keeps it true.
- Real order and timing of the parts differs by region and driller. The video shows one common order, not the only one.
- Left out on purpose: well cap, pitless adapter, check valve, pressure switch.
- "Whenever you pour a glass" is a rough description of a system with no storage tank.
- A real well is built by a licensed driller under local rules. The video is an explanation, not a how-to. The description carries one line saying so.
- "Before dinner" and "the hole fails 5 ways" in the hook are a joke and our own count of the five problems shown after the water is found; they are not technical claims. The 43 million figure is for the US only, so the description says so.

## Cut for lack of two sources

- Nothing in the final narration was cut. Earlier draft hook lines such as "most owners can name none of them" were removed because they are unsourced claims about people.
