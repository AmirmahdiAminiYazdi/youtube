## ۳. توضیحات

‏خط اول ۱۳۴ نویسه است. ۱۲۵ نویسه‌ی اول در پیش‌نمایش دیده می‌شود و کلیدواژه در ۲۵ کلمه‌ی اول است.

```
How a water well works, built in 3D one part at a time: borehole, casing, grout seal, well screen, submersible pump and pressure tank.
Each part fixes the problem the last one created. More than 43 million people in the US drink from private wells (USGS, EPA).

1. Borehole: drilled down to an aquifer, a layer of sand, gravel or rock that holds groundwater.
2. Casing: a steel or plastic pipe that stops loose ground from caving in.
3. Grout seal: cement or clay in the gap around the casing, so surface water can't run down the outside.
4. Well screen: a slotted pipe that lets water in and holds sand back, used in sandy ground.
5. Submersible pump: sits below the water level and pushes water up to the house.
6. Pressure tank: stores water under pressure, so the pump doesn't start for every glass.

Simplified on purpose: the order of the parts varies by region and driller, many wells drilled into solid rock have no screen, and the well cap, pitless adapter, check valve and pressure switch are left out. "Before dinner" is a joke, not how long drilling takes.

Real wells are built by licensed drillers under local rules. This is an explanation, not a how-to.

Narration is an AI-generated voice. The 3D animation was made by Brainmaxx.

Sources: USGS Water Science School, "Aquifers and Groundwater"; USGS, "What's in Your Well Water?"; US EPA, "About Private Water Wells" and the Private Well module; University of Arizona Cooperative Extension, "Private Water Well Components" (AZ1486b, AZ1504); University of Nebraska-Lincoln Extension G2152; Washington State WAC 173-160-231.

Brainmaxx: Think better. Learn faster.
```

```
Is 43 million real?
Yes, for the US: USGS and EPA estimate more than 43 million people, about 15 percent, drink from private wells.

Does every well have a screen?
No. Screens are for sandy or gravelly ground. Many wells drilled into solid rock stay an open hole below the casing.

Why not just put the pump at the top?
A pump at the surface can only pull water up a limited height. A submersible pump sits in the water and pushes it up, which works for deep wells.

Can I build my own well like this?
Please don't use this as a guide. Wells are built by licensed drillers and local rules apply.
```
