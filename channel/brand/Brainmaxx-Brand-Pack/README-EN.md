## File choice
- color-dark: teal mark, off-white rings/type, orange nodes. Use on navy or dark backgrounds.
- color-light: teal mark, navy rings/type, orange nodes. Use on off-white or light backgrounds.
- white: single-color off-white, for dark backgrounds.
- navy: single-color navy, for light backgrounds.
- signature: horizontal mark + wordmark + tagline.
- horizontal: mark + wordmark, without tagline.
- stacked: vertical arrangement with tagline.
- digital-glow: controlled SVG glow for digital display; keep flat files for printing.

## Production details
The approved raster mark was traced and cleaned into genuine vector paths. The digital glow raster reference and earlier banner are preserved as separate assets; flat vector surfaces do not reproduce their original texture. All SVG wordmarks are outlined. They use Outfit Bold, with Outfit Regular support copy; this is a deliberate typeset companion to the generated banner wordmark, not a claim to recover its original font. Every vector mark preserves four orange nodes and five terminal rings.

Large transparent master PNGs are rendered from vector at 4096 px. They are not upscaled raster masters. Profile files include the clean vector-derived version and the approved earlier glow version. 16-32 px icons are utility files: fine detail is reduced by downsampling.

Colors are sRGB. No CMYK values are supplied because conversion must follow the actual printer/material ICC profile. This pack does not provide trademark clearance. Fonts include their SIL Open Font License.
